Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries

Back to Home
What classes does the school offer?
Residential Shanti Niketan offers education from Pre-Nursery to Class 8, covering Pre-Primary (Pre-Nursery, Nursery, LKG, UKG), Primary (Class 1-5), and Middle School (Class 6-8).
How can I apply for admission?
You can fill the admission enquiry form on our homepage, or contact the school office directly at 9155658081. Our team will guide you through the admission process.
How can I pay the school fees?
Fees can be paid online through our Parent/Student Portal using UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) by scanning the QR code or clicking the UPI payment link. Payment history is also tracked in your dashboard.
How do I get my login credentials?
Login credentials are created by the school admin. You will receive your Unique ID (e.g., TSNV00001, PSNV00001, STSNV00001) and a default password. On your first login, you will be asked to set a new password.
What are the school timings?
The school operates from Monday to Saturday, with class timings varying by grade. For specific timings, please refer to the class routine or contact the school office.
Is transportation available?
Please contact the school office for information about transportation facilities in your area.
How can parents track their child's attendance?
Parents can log into their Parent Dashboard to view their child's complete attendance record, including present days, absent days, and leave history.
Is the school environment safe for children?
Absolutely! We prioritize student safety with a caring environment, trained staff, and a secure campus. Our core values include safety, respect, and holistic development.
